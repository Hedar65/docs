## How to get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reinstall Windows">
    A clean Windows installation is required before using Redline. Follow the [Windows Reinstall guide](/setup/windows-reinstall) to prepare your system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure BIOS">
    Update your BIOS and enable TPM, Virtualization, and Secure Boot. See the [BIOS Update](/setup/bios-update) and [BIOS Settings](/setup/bios-settings) guides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up Windows">
    Disable Windows Defender, install required drivers and runtimes, and configure network settings. Follow the [Windows Configuration](/setup/windows-configuration) guide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the spoofer">
    [Download the loader](/spoofer/download), log in with your license key, and complete the [spoof process](/spoofer/spoof-steps).
  </Step>
</Steps>
