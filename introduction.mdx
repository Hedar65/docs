## What Mosab Store Spoofer does

When you receive a hardware ban, the game's anti-cheat system records the unique identifiers of your hardware — your HWID. Even if you create a new account, the ban follows your machine. Mosab Store permanently changes those hardware serials so your system is no longer recognized as banned.

Mosab Store targets the identifiers that anti-cheat systems track: TPM chip serials, MAC addresses, monitor serials, disk identifiers, and other system fingerprints.

## Supported anti-cheat systems

Mosab Store is designed to bypass the following anti-cheat systems:

- **RICOCHET** — used by Call of Duty: Warzone and Vanguard, and enforced in Valorant
- **EAC (Easy Anti-Cheat)** — used by Fortnite, Rust, Apex Legends, and hundreds of other titles
- **BE (BattlEye)** — used by PUBG, Rainbow Six Siege, DayZ, and others

## License key and loader

Mosab Store uses a license key-based loader system. After purchase, you receive a key that authenticates your copy of the software. You enter this key in the loader before running the spoof. Keep your key secure — do not share it.
