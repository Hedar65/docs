Before you download the loader, make sure you have completed every step in the setup guide. The spoofer will not work correctly if your system is not properly prepared — skipping setup steps is the most common reason for a failed spoof.

<Warning>
  All steps in the setup guide must be completed before you proceed. Do not download or run the loader until your drivers are installed and your system is ready.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the Loader">
    Go to [loader.cryptauth.net](https://loader.cryptauth.net/) and download the  spoofer loader.

    Save the file to a location you can easily find, such as your Desktop.

    ![سسس](/images/سسس.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run as Administrator">
    Right-click the downloaded loader file and select **Run as administrator**.

    <Warning>
      You must run the loader as Administrator. Launching it normally will cause it to fail or close immediately.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In with Your License Key">
    When the loader opens, enter your license key to log in. Your license key was provided to you at the time of purchase.

    ![Loader login screen](/images/download/03-license-login.png)

    <Note>
      Keep your license key stored somewhere safe. You will need it every time you open the loader.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once you have logged in successfully, you are ready to run the spoof process. Continue to the next step to complete your HWID spoof.

<Tip>
  Proceed to [Complete the HWID Spoof Process](/spoofer/spoof-steps) to run MAC Spoof, TPM Spoof, and PERM Spoof.
</Tip>
