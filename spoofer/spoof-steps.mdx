This page walks you through the complete HWID spoof process using the Mosab loader. Follow each step in order — skipping or reordering steps can result in an incomplete spoof or a continued ban. Before you begin, make sure the loader is already downloaded and you are logged in.

<Warning>
  Do not proceed unless you have completed all setup guide steps and successfully logged into the loader. An incomplete setup will cause the spoof to fail.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Your Current Serials">
    Download [SerialChecker](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KuIgLY_b5hMTKJsARWd1TTOjrhIFAp8m/view?usp=sharing) and run it.

    Note your current hardware serials and take a screenshot. Save the screenshot — you will use it later to confirm that the spoof changed your serials successfully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear TPM">
    Open the TPM management console and clear your TPM chip.

    1. Press **Windows \+ R**, type `tpm.msc`, and press **Enter**.
    2. Click **Clear TPM**.
    3. Click the **Restart** button and wait for your PC to restart fully.

    <Warning>
      Your PC will restart as part of this step. Save any open work before proceeding.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and Run the Loader as Administrator">
    After your PC restarts, download the loader from [loader.cryptauth.net](https://loader.cryptauth.net/) using your license key.

    Right-click the loader and select **Run as administrator**, then log in with your provided key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the PERM Spoof Process">
    Inside the loader, complete the following actions in order:

    1. Log in with your license key.
    2. Type 5 "**MAC Spoof"** and run it.

    3. Type 4 "**Driverless TPM Spoof"** and run it.

    4. Type 1 "**PERM Spoof"** and run it.
    5. Run SerialChecker again and verify that all serials have changed from your original screenshot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Valorant">
    Install Valorant fresh after the spoof is complete.

    <Warning>
      When Windows Firewall asks for permission during installation, click **Cancel** or the **X** button. Do **not** click Allow. Granting firewall access can expose your hardware identity before the spoof is fully registered.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Riot Client Settings">
    Before launching the game, open Riot Client and apply the following settings:

    - Disable **Open Riot Client on Startup**
    - Disable **Hardware Acceleration**
    - Set **Close Window** to **Exit application**

    <Frame>

    </Frame>

    <Note>
      These settings prevent Riot Client from running in the background and reduce the risk of it collecting hardware data at the wrong time.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with a New Account">
    Create a **new account** using an **Incognito** browser window — do not use your banned account.

    Play on the new account for **24 hours**. If you do not receive a VAL 5 error or a ban during that period, your spoof was successful and you can proceed safely.

    <Tip>
      Always test on a fresh account first. Never log back into your previously banned account until you have confirmed the spoof is working.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>
