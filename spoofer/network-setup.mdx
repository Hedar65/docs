If you play games protected by Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC), BattlEye (BE), or RICOCHET, completing the network setup after spoofing is required. These anti-cheat systems can track your network identity in addition to your hardware serials, so this step ensures your network profile is fully refreshed alongside your HWID.

<Note>
  If you use EFI, you can skip this page entirely.
</Note>

## Disable Extra Network Adapters

Leaving extra network adapters enabled can expose additional hardware identifiers. Disable everything except the adapter you are actively using.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Network Connections">
    Press **Windows \+ R**, type `ncpa.cpl`, and click **OK**.

    Note the name of your active network connection before proceeding.

    ![ncpa.cpl active connection](/images/network-setup/01-ncpa-active.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Device Manager">
    Right-click the **Windows logo** in the taskbar and select **Device Manager**.

    ![Open Device Manager](/images/network-setup/02-device-manager.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disable All Inactive Network Adapters">
    In Device Manager, expand the **Network Adapters** section.

    Right-click each adapter and select **Disable device** — disable all of them **except** the one that matches your active connection name you noted in step 1.

    ![Disable inactive adapters](/images/network-setup/03-disable-adapters.png)

    <Warning>
      Do not disable your active adapter. Disabling the wrong one will cut your internet connection.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change Network Adapter Properties

Fine-tuning your adapter's advanced properties removes additional identifiers that anti-cheat software can read.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Network Connections Again">
    Press **Windows \+ R**, type `ncpa.cpl`, and click **OK**.

    ![Open ncpa.cpl again](/images/network-setup/04-ncpa-again.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Adapter Properties">
    Right-click your active connection and select **Properties**.

    In the list of items, enable **only** the following two items and uncheck everything else:

    - **QoS Packet Scheduler**
    - **Internet Protocol Version 4 (TCP/IPv4)**

    Click **OK** to save.

    ![Adapter properties checked items](/images/network-setup/05-adapter-properties.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Advanced Adapter Settings">
    Right-click your active connection again, select **Properties**, then click **Configure**.

    Go to the **Advanced** tab.

    ![Advanced tab](/images/network-setup/06-advanced-tab.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the Required Advanced Settings">
    Find each of the following properties and set them to the values shown:

    ```text
    Advanced EEE             → Disabled
    Network Address          → Not Present
    ARP Offload              → Disabled
    Flow Control             → Disabled
    IPv4 Checksum Offload    → Disabled
    Large Send Offload v2 (IPv6) → Disabled
    TCP Checksum Offload (IPv6)  → Disabled
    UDP Checksum Offload (IPv6)  → Disabled
    ```

    <Note>
      Not all adapters expose every property listed above. Apply the settings that are available and skip any that do not appear.
    </Note>

    Click **OK** to save your changes.

    ![Advanced settings applied](/images/network-setup/07-advanced-settings.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rotate Your IP Address

After spoofing, your IP address may still be flagged. Rotating it ensures your network identity does not carry over from before the ban.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check and Save Your Current IP">
    Visit any IP-checking website, note your current IP address, and take a screenshot. You will compare this against your new IP after the rotation.

    ![Check current IP](/images/network-setup/08-check-ip.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect for 6 Minutes">
    Turn off your internet connection completely for **6 minutes**, then turn it back on.

    Check your IP address again. It should be different from the one you saved.

    ![Disconnect internet](/images/network-setup/09-disconnect.png)

    <Warning>
      If your IP address has not changed, contact your ISP or try the disconnect for a longer period. Do not continue until your IP has rotated.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Cloudflare WARP">
    Download and install [Cloudflare WARP](https://1.1.1.1/) (the 1.1.1.1 app).

    Enable WARP and use it **every time you launch the game** for the first **7 days** after your spoof.

    ![Cloudflare WARP enabled](/images/network-setup/10-warp.png)

    <Tip>
      Cloudflare WARP will not noticeably affect your ping. It is safe to use for gaming and is the recommended VPN for this process.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Why use a VPN for 7 days?

Using a VPN for one week after a ban ensures your network identity is fully refreshed. Some anti-cheat systems track IP-level patterns over time, and a week of clean usage through WARP gives your account enough distance from the original ban. After 7 days, you can disable WARP and play without it.

## Additional Rules

Follow these rules every session to keep your spoof active and functioning correctly:

- **After every restart**, restart your PC **twice** before launching the game.
- **After waking from sleep** or powering on the next day, restart your PC once more before launching the game. This ensures the spoofer activates correctly on boot.
- **Delete the spoofer executable and the `SAVE.txt` file** after you have finished using the tool.
