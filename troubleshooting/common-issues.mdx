---
title: "❗Spoofer Not Opening / Closing Immediately"
description: "Resolve the most frequent  Spoofer problems: missing runtimes, time sync errors, connection failures, and loader permission errors on launch."
---

If the Spoofer is not working as expected, the cause is usually one of a handful of common issues. Work through the fixes below in order to get back up and running quickly.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Download Required Runtimes">
    Your system must have the correct DirectX and Visual C\+\+ Redistributables installed. Missing runtimes are one of the most common reasons the spoofer fails to launch.

    - **DirectX:** [Download DirectX](https://www.microsoft.com/en-US/download/details.aspx?id=35)
    - **Visual C\+\+ Redistributables (All-in-One):** [Download C\+\+ Redistributables AIO](https://www.techpowerup.com/download/visual-c-redistributable-runtime-package-all-in-one/)

    Install both packages, then restart your system before running the spoofer again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Sync Your Time and Date">
    Incorrect time or timezone settings can cause authentication and connection issues with the spoofer.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Time & Date Settings">
        Press `Win + I` to open Windows Settings, then navigate to **Time & Language \> Date & Time**.

        ![Open Date & Time settings](/images/troubleshooting/time-01-settings.png)
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enable Automatic Time Zone">
        Make sure **"Set Time Zone Automatically"** is turned **ON**.

        ![Set Time Zone Automatically](/images/troubleshooting/time-02-auto-tz.png)
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      Even if your clock looks correct, disabling and re-enabling the automatic setting forces Windows to re-sync with the time server.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use Cloudflare Warp 1.1.1.1">
    Connection or region-based restrictions can prevent the spoofer from reaching its servers. Cloudflare Warp routes your traffic through Cloudflare's network and often resolves these issues.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Download Cloudflare Warp">
        Get the Warp client from the official page: [Download Cloudflare Warp](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/connections/connect-devices/warp/download-warp/)

        ![Download Cloudflare Warp](/images/troubleshooting/warp-01-download.png)
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enable Warp">
        Open the Warp client and toggle it **on** before launching the spoofer.

        ![Enable Warp](/images/troubleshooting/warp-02-enable.png)
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Run Loader as Administrator">
    The spoofer loader requires elevated privileges to access system-level hardware identifiers. Without administrator rights, it will fail silently or close immediately.

    <Warning>
      Always run the spoofer loader as Administrator. Running it without elevated privileges will cause it to fail.
    </Warning>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Right-click the spoofer file">
        Locate the spoofer loader executable in File Explorer.

        ![Locate spoofer file](/images/troubleshooting/admin-01-locate.png)
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Run as Administrator">
        Right-click the file and choose **"Run as Administrator"** from the context menu.

        ![Run as Administrator](/images/troubleshooting/admin-02-run-as-admin.png)
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      To avoid repeating this step, right-click the file, go to **Properties \> Compatibility**, and check **"Run this program as an administrator"**.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
